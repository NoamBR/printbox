## Global Style Notes (Apply to all branded mockups)
- **Color discipline**: matte black `#0A0A0A` base, polished gold foil `#C9A55A` typography/accents only — no other colors.
- **Lighting**: cinematic side or 45° directional light; foil must register specular highlights against matte body.
- **Background**: dark charcoal gradient for branded shots, absolute white `#FFFFFF` for clean shots.
- **Camera**: 50–85 mm equivalent; three-quarter hero angle unless noted.
- **Negative prompt (recommended)**: `text artifacts, logos of real brands, glitch, low-res, oversaturation, plastic-looking foil, watermark`
